# Getting started

## The panel

The panel opens at `/admin` on your site. The menu is on the left:

The first time you sign in, a **tour** shows you the panel one area at a time: the overview, the request to the AI, the things to do, content, releases and newsletter, media, settings, search with ⌘K, and your profile. Move through it with ← → and close it with Esc. You can find it again in the profile menu at the top right (**Panel tour**), or by searching for "tour" with ⌘K. When you open a page, another tour introduces the editor.

- **Overview**: opens on what needs doing. At the top you see the date and a summary. With AI set up, there is the **What shall we do on the site today?** field (see [AI and MCP](09-ai-and-mcp.md#requests-from-the-overview)). On a new site, administrators see **First steps**: complete the site details, publish the first page, write the first article, connect the AI and invite the people you work with. Each step ticks itself off once done, and the box goes away when they are all done; its close button hides it sooner. Then come the **To do** cards: documents waiting to be published, the release in progress, the next scheduled release, and new messages. Below are **Latest changes**, **The site at a glance** with the numbers for each collection, and **Quick actions**. At the top are **Open the site**, **Site from a brief**, and **New page**.
- **Content**: the site's collections. Every site has at least **Pages**. Other collections (for example Projects, Journal, Team) depend on how the site is built.
- **Media**: uploaded images, videos, and files.
- **Settings** and **People**.

At the top left, **Visit the site** opens the published site. At the bottom left is the content language. At the top right, in a notch of the work area, you find search, the light or dark panel theme, and your profile (the circle with your initial). ⌘K (Ctrl+K on Windows) opens search from any screen: pages, settings, commands.

The panel is in English and Italian. The first time, it follows your browser's language; to change it, open your profile (the circle with your initial) → **Your profile** → **Panel language**, or pick the language on the sign-in page. Each person has their own: two people can use the same site, one in English and one in Italian. The panel's messages and the emails Materialize sends you come in the same language. The language of the site's content is a separate thing: you choose it at the bottom left.

## Your first page

1. On the **Overview**, press **New page**, or go to **Pages** and press **New**.
2. Type the **title**. The **address** is created from the title by itself: `About us` becomes `/about-us`. The site's home page is the page with the address `home`.
3. The editor asks **Where do you want to start?**: a page with a few ready-made sections to edit, or **Blank page**.
4. Build the page, as explained in [The page editor](03-editor.md).

You don't need to save. Your changes save by themselves while you work, even if the page isn't finished. They stay a **draft**, though, which only people using the panel can see.

## Publishing

When the page is ready, press **Publish** at the top right.

- If something required is missing (for example the title), the panel doesn't publish and takes you straight to the field to fix.
- After publishing, the site rebuilds by itself, usually in less than a second.
- If you edit a published page again, the button becomes **Publish changes**. The site keeps showing the previous version until you press it.
- To see the page as visitors see it, use the **Open in the browser** icon beside the button: it opens the published version in a new tab.

## Content or Design

At the top of the page there are two ways of working:

- **Content**: write text and change images right on the page, without touching the layout. This is the right mode for people who update text.
- **Design**: build the page on the canvas, with all the editor's tools.

An administrator can decide that editors only use **Content** (see [People and security](08-people-and-security.md)). That way the layout stays the way the agency designed it.

## About Materialize

In **Settings → About** you find the installed version, who makes Materialize, the license, links to the release notes, the guide, and the source code, where to report a problem, and the licenses of the third-party projects included in the panel.
